While a trip is running, every bird ticked in any pentad is added to the trip list with the pentad it was seen in. Moving pentads never removes anything — the trip list is the total of all your ticks. "Download field card" produces a per-pentad summary you can transcribe when submitting cards on the SABAP2 site.
